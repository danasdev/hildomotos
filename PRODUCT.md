# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Inventory lives in one editable data file (`data/estoque.js`) so the owner can add or remove motos without touching layout. Deploys free to Vercel/Netlify/GitHub Pages.

## Users

Buyers in São Luís (MA) and the surrounding island who want a motorcycle, most often for daily work and commuting, many depending on financing. They arrive from Instagram (@hildo_motos, ~50k followers) on a phone, browse what is in stock, and want to talk to a seller on WhatsApp about one specific bike. A smaller group wants to sell or trade in their own bike.

## Product Purpose

The site is the shop window for Hildo Motos: show the current stock (0km and seminovas) in a way that feels premium, and turn interest in a specific bike into a WhatsApp conversation with the right unit. Success = WhatsApp conversations started per bike.

## Positioning

"A loja diferente das iguais." A local, owner-led dealership with two units in São Luís, buying, selling and financing, with on-line and in-person service. Competes with other local resellers (MH Prime Motors, Rex Motos, Imperador Motors) whose presence is Instagram-only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Catalog of motos 0km and seminovas, multimarca.
- Primary CTA: WhatsApp per bike, prefilled with the model, opening the chosen unit's number.
- Financing is offered; no rates, banks, or approval terms confirmed — do not state any.
- Undecided: exact street addresses, opening hours, real prices per bike, which brands are carried.

- Logo: red outlined shield containing an "HM" monogram topped by a crown, "HILDO MOTOS" wordmark with a small winged-helmet mark, all red on black (seen on the real storefront and Instagram avatar; vector file not yet provided).
- Real storefront: black facade, red lettering, interior black wall with a red stripe. The real photo is only a reference (user: not for the site); the site uses an AI-generated facade based on it (assets/img/fachada.jpg).
- User-supplied binding style reference: cinematic red automotive hero, huge display type, black frame with chamfered cut-outs, reeded-glass effect, white rectangular CTA, vertical "scroll" rail. Inspiration: yamaha-motor.com.br (model-centric catalog navigation).

## Product Principles

1. Every bike is one tap from a WhatsApp conversation about that bike.
2. Mobile-first: the visitor comes from Instagram on a phone.
3. Premium presentation, honest content: no fake numbers or claims.
4. The owner must be able to update stock without a developer.
